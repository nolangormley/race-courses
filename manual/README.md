# Traced courses

These GPX files cover events whose organizers publish the course only as a PDF or an image.
An event in `races.yaml` names its file as `source`, and the crawler gives it the same elevation and checks as the other courses.

Each entry below says where its line comes from, how it was traced and how sure it is.
The lines follow OpenStreetMap streets, so they are derived from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).

## pa/pittsburgh-10-miler: EQT Pittsburgh 10 Miler and 10K

- Source: the organizer's 2026 course map on [p3r.org](https://p3r.org/races/eqt-10-miler), a PNG and a PDF without coordinates.
- Method: traced with AI assistance, turn by turn, onto OpenStreetMap streets; between turns the line follows the streets.
- The finish is at Liberty Avenue and 7th Street, in front of EQT Plaza, where P3R puts it.
  Each start is placed by the race distance back from the finish.
  The 10 Miler start then falls on West Carson Street by F.N.B. Stadium, and the 10K start on West General Robinson Street by PNC Park, as P3R describes them.
- The out-and-back crosses the Andy Warhol Bridge to Fort Duquesne Boulevard; the race closed the Andy Warhol and David McCullough bridges in 2024 and 2025.
- Less sure: where on Fort Duquesne Boulevard the turnaround is (about 335 m west of 7th Street), the short way under the Fort Duquesne Bridge on North Shore Drive, and Western Avenue and Fulton Street between the West End Bridge and Reedsdale Street.
- Check: mile markers 2, 3, 4, 5 and 8 fall where the map shows them; markers 1, 6, 7 and 9 fall 150 to 350 m before the map's markers.
- The map's elevation chart agrees on the West End Bridge (at 1.9 to 2.0 miles) and roughly on the David McCullough Bridge, but it puts the Andy Warhol Bridge crossings about half a mile later than this trace.
  The drawn route, its arrows and mile markers, and P3R's description of the 10K start agree with the trace.
